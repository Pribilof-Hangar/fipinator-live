FIPinator Live runs realistic gauges on Logitech (Saitek) Flight Instrument Panels while you fly
Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024. It's free and open source (AGPL-3.0).

What you need:
- Windows 11
- Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024
- The Logitech Flight Instrument Panel driver (free from Logitech; this installer offers the link if
  it's missing)

That's all: FIPinator Live carries everything else it needs, including Microsoft's SimConnect.dll
(the file that lets apps talk to the sim, from the MSFS 2024 SDK).

Using SPAD.neXt for your FIPs? Only one program can drive the FIPs at a time: switch off SPAD.neXt's
FIP support while you use FIPinator Live (FIPinator never changes SPAD's settings). Prefer to stay
with SPAD.neXt? There's a free set of FIPinator gauges made for it, no FIPinator Live needed.

Installs for your Windows user only (no administrator rights). Your panels and gauges are kept in
Documents\FIPinator 3000, and uninstalling never deletes them.
