# About the name

"FIPinator 3000" is meant to be silly, like a late-night TV gadget. Don't take it seriously.

The Logitech (Saitek) FIPs are old hardware that isn't sold new any more, and many of us have a few sitting
around. This is just a fun, free way to give them a second life: no paid gauge packs, no subscriptions, nothing
to buy. Just for fun.
